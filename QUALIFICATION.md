# AI business owner qualification

## Target
AI software founders, implementation/automation business owners, or specialist consultants with a concrete offer and demonstrated domain expertise. Include occasional YouTube publishers and owner-led businesses where educational video has a plausible customer-acquisition role. The latter is a hypothesis, not proof of interest.

## Decision rules
- Research candidate: identified human owner + AI business + at least one source to investigate.
- Evidence-backed candidate: primary-source offer and expertise evidence, with dates, direct excerpts and links.
- Revenue-supported: direct, dated revenue evidence tied to the correct person/company. Self-reported company revenue stays self-reported company revenue, never personal income, profit, or available marketing budget. Third-party interviews repeating a founder's number remain self-reported. Funding, pricing, followers and customer logos are not revenue verification.
- Outreach-ready: human verifies current activity, plausible offer fit, lawful/permitted contact route, suppression/prior-contact status, message claims and ability to fulfill. Never automatically inferred from a score.

## YouTube
Use occasional only after dated recent channel uploads establish the cadence. A channel link alone does not. A guest appearance proves appearance, not ownership. No channel found in a bounded search means none_found, not proof no channel exists. Unknown is preferable to invented cadence.

## Priority order
1. Real owner and identifiable relevant offer.
2. Demonstrated expertise: working demos, technical articles, product work, substantive teaching.
3. Credible dated business-revenue evidence, distinguishing source and entity.
4. YouTube relevance: clear audience overlap and useful repeatable teaching topics.
5. Explicit recent content/growth/production signal if present.
6. Plausible bounded service opportunity, separately labeled as hypothesis.

No claiming low views mean bad business results. No presuming irregular uploads imply frustration. No inferred personal finances. Reject deceptive products and undisclosed promotional accounts; do not invent legitimacy from polished marketing.

## X-first review
- Daily sourcing goal: about 30 relevant new prospects, without padding weak candidates to hit quota. A verified personal X account is a bonus, not a qualification requirement. Keep suitable businesses with unknown or unverified X identities eligible for research and campaigns; confirm the contact route before outreach. A business account is not interchangeable with its founder.
- `x_status=verified` means identity matched through an owner-controlled source linking that account. It does not mean X blue-check verification, current activity, DM eligibility, permission to contact, or buying intent.
- Never guess an X handle from a name. If unresolved, show unknown/not found and retain the bounded-search limitation. Search snippets alone are insufficient identity evidence.
- Direct X collection remains unavailable until the Browser Bridge is connected and read-only retrieval succeeds. Public web search plus primary-site profile links is the currently available discovery path.
- Keep credible businesses with missing income or intent in the research queue, not a sales-qualified or ready-to-DM list.

## Review loop
Choose shortlist/maybe/reject/do_not_contact and record why. Human feedback refines future searches; it does not alter original source claims. Never contact suppressed people through a different channel. No scheduling or automated outreach in V1.

## Initial operating cadence
Run a small discovery batch manually, inspect primary sources, append reviewed evidence records to the import file, import transactionally, review dashboard, draft one specific helpful message only after approval. Do not scale volume before relevance is validated. Track accepted candidates and qualified conversations, not just search-hit volume.
